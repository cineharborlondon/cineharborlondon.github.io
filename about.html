What is
Cine Harbor?

Cine for cinema.
Harbor for a place to come together.

A meeting point for people, ideas and stories. Bring us a project, and we’ll help you find the right way forward.

Cine Harbor is a London-based creative production studio for commercial film, storytelling and photography.

From brand campaigns and short films to social content and events, we bring creative development, production and post-production together. We shape the right team for each brief, large or small.
