What is
Cine Harbor?

Cine for cinema.
Harbor for a place to come together.

A meeting point for people, ideas and stories. We bring together directors, cinematographers, lighting crews and post-production collaborators, shaping the right team for each project, large or small.

Come to us with an idea, a brief or a question. We can make it happen with our own team, or connect you with the right people to take it further.

London · Europe · US

Cine Harbor is a London-based creative production studio working across commercial film, social content, branded storytelling, events and photography.

From fast-moving social campaigns and live coverage to cinematic brand films and narrative-led productions, we build flexible teams around each brief — combining creative development, production and post-production in one streamlined workflow.

Our work spans brands, agencies, cultural organisations and public figures across the UK, Europe and the US.
